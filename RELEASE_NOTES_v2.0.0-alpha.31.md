# Submarine Commander WW2 — v2.0.0-alpha.31

## Phase 16 — Dynamic War Events

This phase adds dynamic national war events to make each campaign react to campaign progress, strategic pressure, intelligence, decryption and high command choices.

### Highlights
- Germany: Atlantic opportunity windows, B-Dienst breakthroughs, closing air gap, escort carrier response and final hunt pressure.
- United Kingdom: Western Approaches progress, U-boat surges, HF/DF tracking, air cover and escort fatigue.
- United States: torpedo crisis, MAGIC intelligence, forward tenders, radar refits and tightening blockade.
- Active events now influence theater risk, pressure, readiness, intelligence and projected tonnage.
- Players can register active events in the strategic journal.
