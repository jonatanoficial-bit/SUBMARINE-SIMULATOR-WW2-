# Release Notes — v2.0.0-alpha.11

## Phase 11 — Independent National Campaigns

### Added
- Three independent national campaigns:
  - Germany / Kriegsmarine — Atlantic Wolfpacks.
  - United Kingdom / Royal Navy — Mediterranean and Allied Interdiction.
  - United States / US Navy — Pacific Patrols.
- 24 campaign missions, eight per nation.
- Campaign overview with base, chronology, doctrine, strategic goal, enemy force and progress.
- Nation-filtered mission list.
- Campaign-specific briefing intelligence.
- Campaign progression that unlocks only the next mission in the same nation.
- New campaign validation and browser smoke tests.

### Preserved
- Mobile scroll stabilization.
- Real depth and speed instruments.
- Physics, sensors, TDC, naval AI, tactical encounter, environment, training and difficulty systems.
- Save schema v3 and autosave operation support.
- Three languages: Portuguese, English and Spanish.
