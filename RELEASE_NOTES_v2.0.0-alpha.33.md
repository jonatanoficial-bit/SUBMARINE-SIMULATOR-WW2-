# Release Notes — v2.0.0-alpha.33

## Phase 18 — Cadeias de Operações Especiais

This release adds chained special operations to the three independent campaigns. Germany, the United Kingdom and the United States now have progressive strategic operation chains, with prerequisites, costs, persistent effects and campaign/strategy UI panels.

## Highlights

- Four-step chained operation arc per nation.
- Sequential unlocks based on mission progress, previous steps, active war events and launched special operations.
- Persistent effects for intelligence, decryption, ASW risk, strategic pressure, readiness, projected tonnage, morale and fatigue.
- Anti-duplication save lock and logbook history.
- Full PT-BR, EN and ES UI coverage.
